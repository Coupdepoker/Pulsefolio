Pulsefolio started as a personal project driven by passion — and a simple need: better understand and manage my own finances.

Existing services either charge too much or don't match my vision. Names like Michael Burry generate headlines with bold bets, but that's speculation. My conviction is different: it's not about chasing returns, it's about intelligently protecting your capital.

I already have very promising first results that I wanted to share. There's still a lot of work ahead — improving performance, reducing risk, increasing robustness. I'll keep you updated as the project evolves.

5 minutes a month. That's it.
5 minutes par mois. C'est tout.

Positions are adjusted on the 1st of each month — it takes about 5 minutes. No day trading, no constant monitoring, no stress. A simple, disciplined process that fits any schedule.

Les positions sont ajustées le 1er de chaque mois — cela prend environ 5 minutes. Pas de trading intraday, pas de surveillance constante, pas de stress. Un processus simple et discipliné adapté à tous.

Dynamic crisis protection
Protection dynamique anti-crise

When equities weaken, the portfolio automatically rotates to defensive assets. It absorbs the shock while staying invested for the recovery.

Quand les actions faiblissent, le portefeuille bascule automatiquement vers des actifs défensifs. Il absorbe le choc tout en restant en position pour la reprise.
